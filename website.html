"Before CARE built our water pump, I walked four hours every day. Now I use that time to study."

Born from a world in crisis. Built for a world that can do better.

In 1945, twenty-two American organizations came together to send
CARE Packages® to survivors of World War II. Those first packages of
food and supplies were a simple act of human solidarity — and they
sparked a movement that has never stopped.

Today CARE operates in over 100 countries, partnering with local communities to
deliver emergency relief and build lasting change. We put a special focus on
women and girls — because we know that lifting one woman lifts an entire
community.

"We will not rest until every person lives in dignity and security."

— CARE International founding principle
